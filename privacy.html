Alpha60 tools · Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

Alpha60 tools is used only by its owner. With the owner's consent it accesses the owner's own Google Drive files through Google's APIs, solely to copy them between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. It does not collect, store, share, sell or transfer any data to anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: the developer contact email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
